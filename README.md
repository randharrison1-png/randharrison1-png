## What I'm Working On

### Automated Trading System
A Python-based trading platform designed to research and analyze short-term market opportunities.

**Technologies:**

`Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Redis` `Alpaca API` `Market Data APIs` `OpenAI API`

The system focuses on:

- Market data analysis
- Trading signals
- Historical backtesting
- Risk management
- Automated trade execution
- News and catalyst analysis
- Trade logging and analytics
